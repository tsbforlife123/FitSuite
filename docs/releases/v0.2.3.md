- Your fix is used, not just shown: after you measure, the blind test starts by itself, and a fix that makes you faster is switched on in your games through Raw Accel. The Mouse tab is now two boxes: "1 Measure and fix" and "2 Use it in your games". The quick check moved to Settings → Your mouse.
- New: sensitivity that changes with hand speed. If your near flicks land long and your far flicks land short (or the other way round), the fix adds a gentle speed curve (at most 6%) that Raw Accel applies on every mouse movement. It only reacts to how fast your hand moves, never to anything in the game.
- Measure with your main game: pick the shooter you play most (VALORANT, CS2, Apex, Overwatch 2, and others). The test turns like that game, and the setup shows where to find its sensitivity.
- Calibration: round 3 is now "Returning flicks". Tracking is easier: a bigger, slower dot that glides instead of jerking, with a faint trail that still counts as on target. No score during the test, just how far through the round you are. A thin trail behind the crosshair shows each flick's path, and the aim report says how straight your flicks are.
