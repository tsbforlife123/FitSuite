- Install in one click: download **FitSuite-Setup.exe** and click Install. No administrator rights needed. It adds FitSuite to the Start menu and the desktop, and you can uninstall it from Windows Settings > Apps. Your settings and measurements are kept.
- Your fix now reaches Raw Accel by itself. If Raw Accel hasn't saved its settings yet, FitSuite writes Raw Accel's default settings file, so you don't have to open Raw Accel first.
- The test shows your main game's view: its own field of view (VALORANT 103°, Counter-Strike 2 106.26°, other games the value you enter), shaped to your monitor.
- Search about 200 gaming mice by maker or model. Mice with checked specs warn you, right where you start measuring, if your DPI or report rate doesn't fit.
- Settings > Windows settings for games: see Game Mode, GPU scheduling, power plan and pointer precision, with a button to open each in Windows. FitSuite only reads them.
- Updates are checked automatically every time FitSuite opens. Choose Update or Not now, or turn on "Install updates by themselves" to have them installed the next time FitSuite opens.
- These notes open each time FitSuite opens until you click "Don't show again". "What's new" in the bottom-right corner opens them any time.
