- Live tuning: after your fix passes the blind test, it keeps learning from your real flicks while you play. FitSuite judges each flick by how you finished it (good, overshot, undershot or sideways) and nudges the fix live when some directions keep landing short or long: at most 1% per change, at most every 3 minutes, never past the fix's limits, your average unchanged.
- The Mouse tab shows how many flicks were judged and how many were good, with "Back to the measured fix" to undo every nudge. Untick "Keep tuning the fix while I play" to stop it.
